# Feature 2: Household Management

Group members into households so family contact and address details stay consistent.

## Goals

- Create, view, edit, and archive households
- Link members to a household
- Share a primary address and household contacts
- Move members between households without losing profile data

## Household fields

- Household name
- Primary address
- Primary phone
- Head of household
- Linked members
- Notes

## Out of scope

- Attendance tracking
- Giving / donations
- Event registration
